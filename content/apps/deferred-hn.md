I don't want to check HN all day every day, but I also don't want to have FOMO.

This little thing here collects top 5 interesting posts from the frontpage of HN every day during the week, and presents them as a little digest every Saturday at 10am (Berlin time). You can sit down with a drink and have a quiet browse.
